# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
community. Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, give actionable
feedback, and respect privacy and confidentiality.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, publishing another person's private information, or sharing Codex
prompts, transcripts, credentials, and machine data without explicit permission.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, review
threads, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and will avoid
conflicts of interest when reviewing them.
